The Journey

Follow the Pillar. A pillar of cloud by day and fire by night stands over the next station. The compass at the top of the screen points to it. Reach the station to hear its story and receive its blessing — then the pillar moves on.

Survive. Hunger, water, warmth, stamina and faith all matter. Manna feeds you; the waterskin carries water; fire at Camp or the Temple restores warmth; the Sabbath restores faith.

Walk: WASD or arrow keys. Hold Shift to run while stamina lasts.

Gather: Touch the glowing manna flakes. Gather your household quota first — it is reserved at dusk. Surplus can be traded.

Actions: F eat manna · R drink from your waterskin · E fill the waterskin at water or warm at a fire.

The Camp: The people need food, water and leadership. At nightfall you may confront the Golden Calf, lift the Bronze Serpent, or trade the surplus. Neglect them and faith falls.

Forty Years: Each day is a year of the journey. Reach all eight stations before the fortieth year ends. If your wellbeing reaches zero, the journey ends.

Your Tribe sets how you travel and how much manna falls; your Household size then makes the journey easier or harder.

APIs & Data

Terrain pilot: Wadi Rum, Jordan.

Real elevation, when available, comes from Mapzen Terrarium / SRTM tiles hosted on AWS Open Data.

The map basemap uses Esri World Imagery (satellite, no API key). If external tiles are blocked, the map falls back to the explored-section relief drawing.

If external elevation tiles are blocked, the game uses a deterministic Wadi Rum terrain model generated in-engine.

3D Models

World, Moses, camp, stations and pickups are built from procedural Three.js geometry.

Blender sources and GLB exports live in models/blender and models/glb: wanderer, tent, campfire, market stall, palm, acacia, rock, split rock, shrine, oasis, manna, water and banner.

Runtime currently uses the procedural versions; the GLBs are retained for future integration.
