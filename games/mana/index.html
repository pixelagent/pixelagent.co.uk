The Wilderness Law

Each dawn the manna falls upon the desert. Gather it, but heed this: manna spoils — what is not sold by nightfall is wasted and lost.

Walk: Use arrow keys, WASD, or the on-screen controls. The journey itself wearies you — walking slowly drains your wellbeing, so move with purpose.

Gather: Touch the glowing manna to add it to your basket.

Trade: Visit the Market to learn the day's price. The price is only set once your gathering is done — some days the camp is generous, other days every soul is selling and the price falls to nothing.

Wellbeing & Wealth: These are two separate fates. Gather too little and you go hungry — wellbeing falls. Gather too much and cannot sell it all — the waste also costs you wellbeing. Sell well and your wealth grows, but wealth alone cannot save a starving soul. Gather exactly what is needed, sell it all, and you may survive forever.

Places: Explore to find the Camp, the Oasis, Split Rock, the Temple, and the Wilderness beyond.

Your Tribe sets how you travel and how much manna falls; your Household size then makes the journey easier or harder.
