How to Play

Survive in the desert by collecting mana orbs each day. Each mana orb gives you score but also makes you sick.

Move: Use arrow keys, WASD, or the on-screen mobile controls to walk across the desert.

Collect: Touch the blue mana orbs to gain points and survive.

Avoid Sickness: Too much mana makes you sick. Manage your health carefully.

Health: You recover +15 health between days. If your health hits zero, it is game over.

Locations: Explore the map to find landmarks like Camp, Oasis, Split Rock, Temple, and Wilderness.

Difficulty: Easy / Normal / Hard changes the amount of mana and your speed.
