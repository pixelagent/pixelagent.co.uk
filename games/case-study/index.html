Case Study

Holy Week

A multi-platform investigative narrative built around testimony, evidence and player deduction. Explore the same story world through desktop, mobile and experimental tool interfaces.

The Challenge

Designing for Truth

How do you turn a well-known narrative into an experience that respects its gravity while inviting active participation? The goal was to create an interactive story that rewards close reading, careful observation and genuine moral reasoning.

3 Platforms
12 Weeks
The Solution

Parallel Interfaces

Instead of a single app, the story was split across complementary experiences: a desktop investigation tool, two mobile narrative paths, and a third experimental interface. Each reveals different facets of the same world.

4 Interfaces
1 Narrative
Results

Measurable Impact

Players moved fluidly between platforms, returning to earlier interfaces with new context. The design encouraged repeat engagement and deeper understanding of the source material.
