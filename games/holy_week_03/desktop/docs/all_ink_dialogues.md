## Act II: The Bethany / Lazarus Conspiracy

### Annas (High Priest Emeritus)
*   **Source File:** `annas_highpriest.ink`
*   **Background:** The elder statesman and former High Priest; the hidden kingmaker.
*   **Story Phase:** The Lazarus Conspiracy and the 'Price of Life' decree.
*   **Bible Verses:** John 11:45-53; John 18:13-24.
*   **Primary Reference:** John 18:13
*   **Prophecies:** Psalm 2:1-2 ("Why do the nations conspire?").
*   **Fulfillment:** Institutional conspiracy against the "monument to power" (Lazarus).
*   **Case Facts:** 
    *   **Culprit:** Institutional decision (Sanhedrin consensus).
    *   **Motive:** Preservation of Temple security and political status quo.

### Martha of Bethany
*   **Source File:** `martha_bethany.ink`
*   **Identity:** A high-truthfulness witness and resilient coordinator.
*   **Story Phase:** Witnessing the physical miracle of Lazarus.
*   **Bible Verses:** John 11:17-44; Luke 10:38-42.
*   **Prophecies:** Isaiah 26:19 ("Your dead will live, LORD; their bodies will rise").
*   **Key Discovery:** The 'Secret Decree' identifying Lazarus as "theological contamination."

### Nicodemus (Conflicted)
*   **Source File:** `nicodemus_conflicted.ink`
*   **Identity:** A member of the Sanhedrin seeking truth in the shadows.
*   **Story Phase:** The secret search for truth; leaking information from within the Council.
*   **Bible Verses:** John 3:1-21; John 7:50-52; John 19:39.
*   **Prophecies:** Jeremiah 29:13 ("You will seek me and find me").
*   **Key Discovery:** Confirmation that the vote to silence Lazarus was weighted beforehand.

### Simon the Leper
*   **Source File:** `simon_leper.ink`
*   **Identity:** A witness to social and physical restoration.
*   **Story Phase:** Preparation for burial and the perfume at Bethany.
*   **Bible Verses:** Matthew 26:6-13; Mark 14:3-9.
*   **Prophecies:** Isaiah 53:3-4 ("Surely he took up our pain and bore our suffering").
*   **Key Discovery:** The presence of Temple spies monitoring the "King's table."
