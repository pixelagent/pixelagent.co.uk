Holy Week

How to Play

Welcome, Scribe. Your task is to investigate the events of Holy Week and uncover the truth behind the mysteries.


1. Explore : Select a location from the map to view available cases.

2. Investigate : Visit the Scene to collect clues. Some evidence is only revealed through conversation!

3. ️ Interview : Talk to witnesses in the People tab. Show them evidence to gather reactions, or Challenge them if you find a contradiction.

4. Analyze : In the Lab, select two pieces of evidence and an operation to find new insights.

5. Prophecies : when you have revealed the prophecies in the Codex. Can you unlock them?

6. Accuse : Once you have enough proof, make your final verdict. Be careful—wrong guesses increase your Doubt.


Scoring System

Earn Points:

🔍 Evidence Collected: +5 points each

🧪 Lab Deductions: +15 (Key) / +8 (Standard)

📜 Prophecy Linked: +10 points each

⚡ Successful Challenge: +10 points each

🎯 Correct Accusation: +50 points

⭐ Perfect Case Bonus: +25 points (no failed challenges)


Lose Points / Gain Doubt:

❌ Incorrect Accusation: -25 points

⚠️ Doubt Penalty: Total Doubt × 2 (subtracted from final score)

💥 Failed Challenge: +10 Doubt

🔬 Incorrect Lab Pairing: +5 Doubt, -5 points

📜 Incorrect Prophecy Link: +5 Doubt


Ranks: Rookie (0-59) → Investigator (60-99) → Analyst (100-149) → Master Detective (150+)
