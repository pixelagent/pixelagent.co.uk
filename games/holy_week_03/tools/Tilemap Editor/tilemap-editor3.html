RANDOM GENERATOR
Background comes from the 🗺️ Old City Map tool (crop the section you want, per case) — this generator handles the rest, in order:
1. Decoration — scattered only onto background tiles flagged "Allow decorations" (Ground, Boundary by default).
2. Collider — synced from background: any tile flagged "Blocks movement" (Water, Wall, Roof, Wall Fill, Boundary by default) gets a collider.
3. NPC spawns — placed only within the single largest connected walkable area, so every placed NPC is guaranteed reachable from every other. Places at least the minimum below (or as many as fit, with a warning, if that area is too small).
