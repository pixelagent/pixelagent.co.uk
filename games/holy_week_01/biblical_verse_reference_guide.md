## Cross-Case Prophecy Threads

These major prophetic currents weave across multiple acts, structurally linking the entire narrative:

### 1. Isaiah 53 — The Suffering Servant Arc
* **Act II (Gethsemane):** Silent before accusers, refusing armed revolution ($v.7$).
* **Act II (Last Supper):** Poured out to death, numbering with transgressors, making intercession ($v.12$).
* **Act IV (Resurrection):** Assigned a rich man's tomb ($v.9$), yet prolonging his days and seeing light after anguish ($v.10–11$).

### 2. Zechariah's Passion Cycle
* **Act I (Entry):** The King arriving humble and mounted on an unridden donkey colt (**Zechariah 9:9**).
* **Act II (Supper):** Weighted out and valued at the slave price of thirty pieces of silver (**Zechariah 11:12–13**).
* **Act II (Garden):** The sword waking against the companion, striking the shepherd so that the sheep scatter (**Zechariah 13:7**).

### 3. Psalm 118 — The Royal Entrance & Foundation
* **Act I (Entry):** The processional liturgical greeting welcoming the King into the city gates ($vv.25–26$).
* **Act III (Temple):** The theological reversal where the cornerstone rejected by the temporary builders becomes the architectural capstone of the true Temple ($vv.22–23$).

## Deeper Studies & Context Passages

For players or investigators wanting to dive into the deeper scriptural framework behind the physical evidence items discovered:

### The Levite & High Priestly Economy
* **Leviticus 5:7 & Exodus 30:13:** Outlines the mandatory sanctuary dues and sacrificial concessions that Caiaphas's family transformed into a predatory corporate bazaar inside the Court of the Gentiles.
* **Mishnaic Record (Shekalim):** Extensively documents the precise markup and coin exchange fees enforced on pilgrims arriving with secular currencies.

### The Passover Seder Elements
* **The Afikomen Custom:** The third unleavened piece broken, wrapped in linen, hidden, and redeemed later in the meal—serving as a precise physical shadow of Jesus's impending body broken and buried in a rock-hewn tomb.
* **The Cup of Redemption:** The third cup poured during the Passover meal, representing God's promise in Exodus 6:6: "I will redeem you with an outstretched arm." This is the exact cup Jesus reframes as the New Covenant in His blood.

### Roman Legal & Security Protocols
* **The Custodia Seal (Matthew 27:66):** The official Roman wax impression strung across tomb entrances. Tampering with or breaking this imperial stamp without governor authorization carried an absolute penalty of execution by crucifixion.
* **The Sanhedrin Secret Fund:** Under Roman oversight, the Temple treasury held explicit jurisdiction over religious infractions but could not legally distribute cash payouts to Roman Imperial soldiers without committing state treason—hence the extreme secrecy of the transaction exposed in Matthew 28:11–15.
