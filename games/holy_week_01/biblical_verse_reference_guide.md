# Biblical Verse Reference Guide
## All Four Cases — Evidence, Prophecy & Deduction Links

This document maps every Bible verse referenced across the four improved case files,
organised by case, then by type: **Prophecy** (Old Testament → fulfilment), **Evidence** (Gospel verses),
and **Deep Dives** (epistles, context passages for investigators who want to go further).

## Cross-Case Prophecy Threads

These OT threads run across multiple cases and connect the whole arc:

### Isaiah 53 — The Suffering Servant
Used across Acts 2, 3, and 4. The chapter describes: silent before accusers (v.7), buried with the rich (v.9), numbered with transgressors (v.12), and prolonging his days after death (v.10–11). It is the most comprehensively predictive OT passage about Jesus.

### Psalm 22 — Crucifixion Psalm
Written ~1000 BC by David. Contains: "My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?" (v.1 — quoted from the cross, Matt 27:46), garments divided by lots (v.18), bones not broken (v.17 context), and ends with "He has done it!" (v.31 — implying future vindication).

### Zechariah 9–14 — The Shepherd-King Cycle
Zechariah 9:9 (donkey entry, Act 1), 11:12–13 (30 silver coins, Act 2), 12:10 (pierced one mourned), 13:7 (shepherd struck, sheep scattered). Four prophecies from one book across four events of Passion Week.

### Psalm 118 — The Coronation Psalm
Used in Act 1 (vv.25–26, the Hosanna cry) and Act 3 (v.22, the rejected cornerstone). The same Psalm echoes twice in Passion Week — first as the crowd's greeting, then as Jesus's prophecy against the religious leaders.
