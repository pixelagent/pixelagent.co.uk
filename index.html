Hi, I’m Nigel — a designer, developer, and educator with a love for animation, games, and storytelling. I teach game design and digital media to young creatives and have over 20 years’ experience building everything from interactive websites to immersive apps.

Right now, I’m focused on building games, web apps, and creative software tools for a new generation. Whether I’m in the classroom or coding a new platformer, I’m all about combining creativity with purpose.
