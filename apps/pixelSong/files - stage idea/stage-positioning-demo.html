💡 Tip: Movement creates a sense of space and energy. Try "Swirling" for dance music or "Call & Response" for dramatic back-and-forth effects!

⏱️ How Movement Works: Instruments move from their START to END position over one complete pattern loop (16/32/64 steps). Higher tempo = faster movement. Each movement style (Smooth, Swing, Bounce) creates different patterns over the loop duration.

🎚️ What Does Front/Back Control?

Front of Stage (0-30%): Dry, close, present - minimal reverb, sounds upfront and intimate
Middle Stage (30-60%): Natural room sound - balanced reverb, typical mixing position
Back of Stage (60-100%): Wet, distant, atmospheric - heavy reverb, creates depth and space

💡 In real audio production, this controls reverb wetness, volume, and high-frequency rolloff to simulate distance. Drums typically stay front (dry), while pads and atmospheres work well at the back (wet).
