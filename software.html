Blender

A collection of custom Blender extensions and tools built to speed up game development workflows - from exporting assets to Unity and A-Frame, to running a playable arcade game directly inside Blender.

Blender

Blender is used here as a development platform beyond just 3D modelling. These custom extensions bridge Blender with game engines and web technologies, making it a versatile hub for building and exporting interactive experiences.



Blender Unity Shader Converter
    Blender addon that automatically converts materials and shaders into Unity-compatible formats. Removes the manual work of recreating materials after export, keeping the visual fidelity of your Blender scene intact when it lands in Unity. github.com/purna/blender_to_unity_shader_converter

    [Still in development]
Download

Blender Unity Exporter

A custom Blender extension that streamlines the process of exporting 3D models and scenes directly into Unity. It handles the common friction points between the two tools - such as scale, axis orientation, and material settings - saving time and reducing the back-and-forth typically involved in getting assets from Blender into a Unity project.

Download
Blender AFrame Exporter
    A Blender add-on that exports 3D scenes directly into A-Frame VR web projects. Convert your Blender environments into browser-ready, WebXR-compatible experiences without manual reformatting - ideal for quickly publishing 3D work to the web. github.com/purna/blender_to_aframe_converter

    [Still in development]

Mobirise

A no-code website builder and custom extension platform - ideal for clients who want to manage their own site, and developers who need a fast, flexible starting point.

Mobirise

One of the simplest solutions for allowing clients to update their own website without ongoing costs. With a drag-and-drop interface and a wide range of polished templates and blocks, it makes it possible to build and maintain professional-looking sites with no coding knowledge - while still giving developers room to extend and customise under the hood.




Global HTML Insert
    A custom Mobirise extension that injects any HTML snippet into the <head> tag across every page of a site simultaneously. Useful for adding tracking scripts, custom fonts, or third-party integrations without editing each page individually.

Meta SEO Insert

A custom Mobirise extension for adding meta descriptions and keywords to the tag of every page. Helps ensure each page is properly indexed by search engines, improving visibility and making the site more competitive in search results.
