About

Hi, I'm Nigel — a solo developer, educator, and creative technologist with a passion for building things that don't exist yet.

With over 20 years of experience in digital media, I've worked on everything from animated websites to mobile apps, from interactive installations to game development tools. These days, I focus on creating browser-based creative tools and games — because I believe the best software comes from solving your own problems.

What I do:
• Game development (Unity, Blender)
• Web development (WordPress, Umbraco, custom solutions)
• Creating browser-based tools for digital artists
• Teaching game design and digital media

Why "Pixelagent"?
The name comes from my love of pixel art and the idea of being an "agent" for pixels — getting them where they need to go. It's also a bit of a nod to my background in animation and game development, where every pixel counts.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me teaching the next generation of creators, experimenting with new technologies, or working on my own game projects.
