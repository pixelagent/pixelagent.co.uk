Pixelagent is an independent creative technology studio based in Brighton, United Kingdom. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it and the choices and rights available to you. It applies to this website and to Pixelagent apps, games, teaching tools and other digital experiences that link to this policy.

1. Who we are

In this policy, “Pixelagent”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to Pixelagent, an independent creative technology studio based in Brighton, United Kingdom. We are the data controller for personal information processed under this policy, unless we say otherwise.

If you have a question about this policy or how your information is handled, please use the contact page and mark your message “Privacy request”.

2. Information we collect

We try to collect only what we need to provide and improve the service. Depending on how you use Pixelagent, this may include:

Information you give us

  • Your name, email address, username, nickname or other profile details you enter.
  • Messages you send through our contact form or by email, including the content of your enquiry.
  • Information you provide in a classroom, workshop, project brief or feedback form.

Information from Google sign-in

If you choose to sign in with Google, we may receive basic account information made available through Google Sign-In or Google Identity Services, such as your name, email address, profile picture, Google account identifier and authentication credentials. We do not receive your Google password. What we receive depends on the permissions you approve on the Google consent screen.

Content you create

When you use a Pixelagent app or teaching tool, we may process the content you create or submit, such as projects, drawings, designs, written answers, game state, scores, room names, team names and files. Some tools store this content locally in your browser rather than on our servers.

Technical and usage information

Our hosting providers may automatically receive technical information such as your IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring page, requested pages, timestamps and error information. We use this to keep services secure, diagnose problems and understand how the site is used.

Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and browser storage where needed to keep you signed in, remember settings, save local work and support interactive features. See Cookies and local storage below.

3. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • authenticate you and maintain your session when you sign in;
  • provide, operate and personalise Pixelagent websites, apps, games and educational tools;
  • save, sync or display your work where a tool offers that feature;
  • support multiplayer, classroom, room-code and collaboration features;
  • respond to enquiries, support requests and privacy requests;
  • maintain security, prevent abuse and troubleshoot errors;
  • understand which resources are useful and improve our services;
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms of service.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use Google user data for advertising, profiling or resale.

4. Google user data

Pixelagent uses Google sign-in to let you access certain services with your Google account. We request only the scopes needed for the feature you are using. For example, basic profile and email access may be used to create or identify your account, display your name and keep you signed in.

Google API Services User Data Policy — Limited Use

Pixelagent’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We do not use Google user data to serve advertising, to build advertising profiles, or to sell information to data brokers. We do not allow humans to read Google user data unless it is necessary for security, legal compliance, support you have asked for, or aggregated and anonymised operational purposes.

You can review or revoke Pixelagent’s access to your Google account at any time in your Google Account permissions. Revoking access may prevent you from signing in to features that rely on Google authentication.

6. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers. Trusted providers that help us host, authenticate, store, secure or support our services, including Google for Google sign-in and cloud services. They may process information only for the purposes we specify.
  • Other participants. If you use a multiplayer or classroom feature, your nickname, team name, game state or submitted work may be visible to other participants in the same room or class.
  • Legal and safety. We may disclose information if required by law, court order or a lawful request, or where necessary to protect the rights, safety or security of Pixelagent, our users or the public.
  • Business changes. If Pixelagent is involved in a merger, acquisition, reorganisation or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to appropriate safeguards.

Third-party services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them, particularly Google’s privacy policy when you use Google sign-in.

7. Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including to provide the service, resolve disputes, maintain security and meet legal obligations.

  • Account information is generally kept while your account or sign-in remains active, and for a reasonable period afterwards if needed to handle deletion, security or legal requirements.
  • Contact enquiries are kept for as long as needed to respond and maintain a record of the conversation, then deleted or archived when no longer needed.
  • Technical logs are kept for a limited period for security and troubleshooting.
  • Local browser data stays on your device until you clear it, use a tool’s reset/delete control, or your browser removes it.

When information is no longer needed, we delete it or anonymise it where deletion is not reasonably possible.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including encrypted connections, access controls and reputable hosting providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong, unique password for your Google account and contact us if you believe your information has been compromised.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

  • access a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • ask us to delete information in certain circumstances;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • ask for information in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent where we rely on it;
  • complain to a data protection regulator.

To make a request, use the contact page and mark your message “Privacy request”. We may need to verify your identity before responding. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

In the UK, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk. We would appreciate the chance to address your concern first.

10. Children and education

Some Pixelagent resources are designed for use in schools, colleges and educational settings. Where a service is used by children, the school, college or teacher is responsible for obtaining any required parental or guardian consent and for supervising use.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate consent. If you believe a child has provided personal information without the required consent, please contact us so we can review and delete it.

11. Cookies and local storage

Pixelagent may use cookies, localStorage, sessionStorage and similar browser technologies to:

  • keep you signed in and maintain a secure session;
  • remember accessibility settings, theme, text size and preferences;
  • save local work, progress or game state on your device;
  • support interactive tools and multiplayer room features;
  • measure basic usage and diagnose errors through hosting or analytics services.

Some storage is essential for a feature to work. Others are optional. You can clear cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Clearing storage may remove locally saved work or sign you out. Where required by law, we will ask for consent before using non-essential cookies.

12. International transfers

Pixelagent is based in the UK. Some service providers, including Google, may process information in other countries. Where information is transferred outside the UK or European Economic Area, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision, where required.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to our services, technology or legal requirements. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. If changes are material, we will take reasonable steps to bring them to your attention. Please check this page periodically.

14. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests or concerns, please contact Pixelagent through the contact page and mark your message “Privacy request”. You can also write to us at Pixelagent, Brighton, United Kingdom.

For general enquiries about our work, the same contact page is the best route.

This policy should be read together with our terms of service. It is provided for transparency and does not create rights beyond those available under applicable law.
